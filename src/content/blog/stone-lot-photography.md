No two amethyst beads are identical. That is the appeal of natural stone — and
the exact reason sample rounds go wrong when suppliers photograph one hero
strand and ship from an unseen parcel.

## Variation is the product

Veining, tone shifts and inclusions are what natural stone is. Lab-created
stones trade that variation for consistency, and both positions are legitimate
— when they are stated. The problem starts when a "natural" listing is quietly
fulfilled from whatever lot was closest to the door.

## Lot photography, as a process

For stone, pearl and gemstone styles, the lot you are buying is photographed
before dispatch: same light, same day, same background. You approve the parcel,
not a representative piece. What you approved is what ships.

## How to specify this in your RFQ

State it in the notes: "photograph the actual lot before dispatch". A supplier
with lot control will confirm in writing; one without will change the subject.
