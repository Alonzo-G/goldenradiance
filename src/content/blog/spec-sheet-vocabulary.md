Fashion jewelry quotes are easy to compare on price and impossible to compare
without five fields. Here is the vocabulary.

## The five fields

1. **Base material grade** — zinc alloy or brass. Weight, rigidity and plating
   adhesion all follow from this.
2. **Plating method** — electroplate (bath) or PVD (vacuum). The process, not
   the color name, predicts wear life.
3. **Plating thickness** — in µm, measured at incoming inspection. Thin plating
   on a ring band is the classic first-season failure.
4. **Dimensions** — stated in mm, per style, so a "6 mm hoop" is 6 mm in every
   listing you build from it.
5. **Weight** — in grams. It is what your shipping quote and your customer's
   first impression are both made of.

## Comparing two quotes

Put the two spec sheets side by side and read down the five fields. If a quote
is missing a field, ask for it in writing before comparing prices — the missing
field is where the difference usually hides.

## Where compliance fits in

For styles sold into the EU, coated articles fall under nickel release limits
enforced after wear simulation. The standard, the limit and the per-batch
reporting model are covered in the compliance center — worth reading before
your first EU order, not after.
