## No two stones are identical

Natural stone, freshwater pearl and lab-created gemstones each carry variation:
veining, tone shifts, inclusions, baroque shape. That variation is the product,
not a defect — and it changes how you should buy.

## Photographed by lot

We photograph the parcel you are buying, not a sample shot. What you approve in
the sample round is what ships with the bulk order.

## Stone dictionary

- **Amethyst** — natural quartz; tone varies by lot.
- **Tiger eye** — chatoyancy varies stone to stone.
- **Howlite (dyed)** — color is stabilized and dyed; stated as dyed, never sold as turquoise.
- **Lapis lazuli** — natural blue with calcite veining.
- **Freshwater pearl** — baroque or near-round; size bands stated per style.
- **Lab-created opal** — consistent color, steel post construction.

## Setting and plating

Mounts and clasps are brass or 316L steel with gold-tone plating or PVD, with
thickness stated in µm. Plating covers findings only on beaded styles — this is
stated per style so you can quote honestly.
