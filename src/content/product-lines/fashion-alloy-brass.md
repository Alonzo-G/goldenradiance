## Breadth and speed for volume buyers

Zinc alloy and brass give the widest styling range at the lowest landed cost. The
trade-off is plating wear, so every style on this line carries a stated plating
method and thickness in microns on its spec sheet — you know exactly what you are
quoting your own customers.

## What we state on every spec sheet

- **Base material grade** — zinc alloy or brass, stated per style.
- **Plating method** — gold-tone, antique, rhodium-tone or matte electroplate.
- **Plating thickness** — in µm, measured at incoming inspection.
- **Weight and dimensions** — measured per style, not estimated.

For coated fashion jewelry sold into the EU, the applicable nickel release limit
is enforced after EN 12472 wear simulation. See the compliance center for what
that means in practice, and how we document it per batch.
