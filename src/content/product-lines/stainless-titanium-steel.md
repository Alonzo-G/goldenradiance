## Built for everyday wear — durability, stated numerically

Stainless and titanium steel are positioned for everyday use: shower, gym, pool.
Whether a given style holds up there depends on the grade and the coating
process, so both are stated numerically on every spec sheet — and corrosion
behavior is quoted per batch with your quotation.

## 316L vs 304

316L adds molybdenum for chloride resistance. It is the grade used for pieces
worn against skin on this line; 304 is not substituted without saying so.

## PVD vs water plating

PVD bonds the layer in a vacuum chamber; water plating deposits it in a bath.
PVD resists wear longer, and the thickness in µm is stated on every style.

## Corrosion, explained

Salt-spray behavior is a test result, not a promise. Every batch is sent for
testing and the report travels with your quotation — ask for it in the RFQ
notes and it is attached to your quote.
