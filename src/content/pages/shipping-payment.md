## MOQ rules

MOQ 12-120 pcs, stated per style. Quantities below a style's MOQ can be quoted
— flagged for confirmation at submission.

## Tiered pricing

Priced by tier — see each collection. The exact tier table is quoted per order;
the reference range on each product page is the band that matches the style's
MOQ.

## Lead time

Lead time quoted per order.

## Delivery terms

- **EXW** — you collect from our warehouse.
- **FOB** — we deliver to the vessel at the named Chinese port.
- **DDP** — we handle transport and import duty to your door.

## Carriers & transit

Carrier options and transit times are quoted per order and destination.

## Payment

Payment methods and terms are stated with your quotation.

## Defects & claims

Defects are replaced or credited after photo review — the claim window is
written into your order confirmation.
